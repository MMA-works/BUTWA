University Capstone & Research Initiative

About BATWA & The Founders

BATWA started as an ambitious computer science university project. We didn't build a commercial app — we built the vision of what digital banking in Pakistan should truly feel like.

Our Origin & Mission

Why We Built BATWA

For years, consumers in Pakistan have dealt with slow, complicated banking apps burdened with high latency, hidden service charges, and fragile network recovery.

As undergraduate computer science students, we chose to dedicate our capstone project to solving this challenge from first principles:

01 / Sub-50ms Settlement

Instant Raast payment framework simulation with transactional idempotency.

02 / Memory-Hard Argon2id

Hardware-enclave MPIN hashing immune to modern GPU cracking attacks.

03 / 3D Titanium NFC Emulation

Contactless virtual card simulation with instant freeze and CVV masking.

04 / Zero-Latency OTA Updates

Direct in-app continuous update engine bypassing app store rollout delays.

BATWA is our proof-of-concept for how clean, secure, and beautiful sovereign banking can be when built with care.

Academic Prototype Notice: BATWA is an educational university research project created for academic evaluation. It is not an active SBP-licensed commercial banking institution and does not hold real customer fiat deposits.
