University OOP Course Project • Research Prototype

The Story Behind BATWA

BATWA was designed and developed as a university Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) project. We wanted to challenge legacy financial systems and build the vision of what digital banking in Pakistan should truly feel like.

Why We Built This Project

"We didn't build a commercial app — we built the vision of what banking should be."

Traditional banking applications in Pakistan are often sluggish, complex, burdened with hidden fees, and hindered by fragmented interfaces. As computer science university students, we asked ourselves a simple question:

"What if a digital wallet settled Raast transactions in under 50ms, offered simulated titanium NFC contactless payments, verified biometric MPINs with memory-hard Argon2id encryption, and updated itself without relying on app store delays?"

BATWA is our response to that challenge. While not an active commercial bank, it is a fully functioning technical realization of clean object-oriented domain modeling, high-concurrency ledgering, and intuitive user experience.

University Project Disclosure: BATWA is an educational university course project demonstrating OOP principles, RESTful microservices, and cryptographic security patterns. It does not solicit deposits or commercial banking activities.
